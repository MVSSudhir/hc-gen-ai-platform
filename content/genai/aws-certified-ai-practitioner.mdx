The AWS Certified AI Practitioner (AIF-C01) is aimed at people who use AI/ML on AWS more than they implement training pipelines. Expect questions on problem framing, foundation models, prompt patterns, RAG at a conceptual level, cost and security, and responsible AI. It is not a substitute for shipping retrieval quality or agent evals. Prepare from the official exam guide and AWS Skill Builder paths, then map each domain back to concepts on this site. Recertification is time-bounded; treat the badge as proof you can talk the AWS AI vocabulary with product and security partners.
