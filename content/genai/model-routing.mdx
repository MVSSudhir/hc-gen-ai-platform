Model routers classify intents—"simple FAQ" to small model, "contract analysis" to large—and enforce data policies routing PII-heavy jobs on-prem. Implement shadow mode when introducing routes; compare win rates before cutting over. Log routing decisions for finance chargebacks and debugging quality dips. Shadow-test new routes on sampled production queries before flipping traffic; routing regressions are subtle until finance sees refunds.
