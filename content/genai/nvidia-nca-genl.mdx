NCA-GENL is NVIDIA’s associate certification for generative AI and large language models: prompting, experimentation, and maintaining LLM-enabled applications in the NVIDIA stack. It is remotely proctored with a short multiple-choice format; validity is typically two years. Use NVIDIA’s official certification page for the current blueprint and DLI courses. This path still matters: parametric limits, RAG, and evaluation questions show up regardless of vendor. If you do not work near GPUs or NVIDIA NIM/NeMo-style tooling, a cloud engineer cert on your employer’s stack may be a better signal.
