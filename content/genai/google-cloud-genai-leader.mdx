Generative AI Leader tests whether you can sponsor GenAI work on Google Cloud: use-case selection, data and privacy implications, and which Vertex AI / Gemini capabilities fit a problem. It is a leadership and literacy exam, not proof you can debug retrieval. Read Google Cloud’s official certification page for domains and recommended training. Pair it with technical partners who own evaluation and serving. If your role is implementation, prefer an engineer certification on the same cloud instead of stacking leader badges.
