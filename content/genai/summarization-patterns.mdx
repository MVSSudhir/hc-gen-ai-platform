Summarization patterns manage length: map-reduce summarizes chunks then merges; refine iteratively updates a running summary; structured outputs force sections—decisions, risks, action items. Pick patterns based on fidelity needs—legal summaries may require extractive anchors. Evaluate with G-Eval style rubrics for omission, not only fluency. Require structured sections—decisions, risks, owners—in executive summaries so readers can skim reliably under time pressure. Validate changes on production-like eval slices before rollout.
