analyticalTypes:

purpose: >
  Compare groups that share a start event over time to isolate lifecycle effects.
businessQuestions:
  - "How does retention differ by hire cohort?"
  - "Did a policy change affect new hire outcomes?"
dataRequired:
  - Clean employee event history
  - Consistent definitions
  - Adequate sample size
method: >
  Group employees by start period or event; track a metric across subsequent intervals.
exampleApplication: >
  Compare 90-day retention for quarterly hire cohorts before and after onboarding redesign.
interpretation: >
  Parallel cohort trends suggest external factors; diverging trends suggest intervention impact.
limitations: >
  Small cohort sizes; confounding when hiring profile changes between cohorts.
